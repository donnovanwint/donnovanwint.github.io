CLIENT REVIEW

Primary CTA Button — Style Options

Four directions for the site's main "Schedule a Tour" / "Apply Now" button. Try each accent color below to see how it looks across all four styles.

ACCENT COLOR
A. Pill, Solid Fill CURRENT PICK
Fully rounded, solid fill, soft drop shadow. Reads friendly and approachable — the shape a hand-drawn "come on in" invite would take.
Schedule a Tour
Hover: darkens one shade + lifts 1px with a deeper shadow.
B. Rounded Rectangle
Slight 10px radius, solid fill. More structured and institutional — reads a touch more "official school form" than the pill.
Apply Now
Hover: darkens one shade, no movement — calm and steady.
C. Outline, Inverts on Hover
Transparent with a colored border; fills solid only on hover. Feels lower-pressure and confident — good as a secondary tour CTA next to a bolder primary.
Schedule a Tour
Hover: border and text invert into a solid fill — a little "reveal" moment.
D. Soft Tint Fill
A light tint of the accent color with matching text, no border. Quiet and warm at rest, then commits to full color on hover — least "salesy" of the four.
Request Info
Hover: tint fills to full solid color, text turns white.
WHY THIS MATTERS FOR HVCA

A pill shape and a warm accent color read as an invitation, not a hard sell — important for parents making an emotional, unhurried decision about their child's school. Outline and tint styles work well as secondary actions ("Request Info") next to a bolder primary ("Schedule a Tour") without competing for attention.
